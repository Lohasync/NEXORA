# NEXORA

## AI Decision Intelligence Copilot

**From Data to Decisions.**

NEXORA is an AI-powered Decision Intelligence Copilot that transforms business data into clear insights, detects important changes, and recommends actions.

---

## 🚨 Problem

Businesses generate large amounts of sales and operational data, but raw data does not explain what is happening or what action should be taken.

Decision-makers need answers to questions such as:

- Why is revenue declining?
- What is causing the change in orders?
- Are customer numbers changing?
- Which business areas require attention?
- What action should be taken next?

NEXORA addresses this gap by combining data analytics, Exasol, and an AI-powered decision layer.

---

## 💡 Solution

NEXORA converts business data into decision-oriented insights.

The system:

1. Loads business data.
2. Stores and analyzes structured data using Exasol.
3. Calculates important business metrics.
4. Detects significant changes and trends.
5. Uses the NEXORA Intelligence layer to interpret those changes.
6. Provides recommended actions through an interactive Streamlit dashboard.
7. Allows users to ask business questions using natural language.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📊 Business Dashboard

Displays important business KPIs:

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Average Order Value

### 📈 Trend Detection

NEXORA analyzes revenue and order trends to identify significant changes.

### 🤖 NEXORA Intelligence

The intelligence layer converts detected business changes into understandable insights and recommended actions.

### 💬 Ask NEXORA

Users can ask questions such as:

- "Why is revenue declining?"
- "What happened to orders?"
- "What should we do?"

NEXORA analyzes the available data and provides a decision-oriented response.

### 🔎 Data Explorer

Users can inspect the underlying business data through the dashboard.
